How to Disable Privacy Mode for Your Telegram Bot (via BotFather)

1. Open Telegram and search for @BotFather (the verified bot with a blue check).
2. Start a chat with BotFather if you aren't already in one.
3. Send the command: /mybots
4. BotFather will list your bots. Select: @FaustoftheBlueRose_bot
5. Choose: Bot Settings
6. Choose: Group Privacy
7. You will see: "Enable" or "Disable". Currently it is likely enabled.
8. Select: Disable (to turn off privacy mode)
9. Confirm the change.

What this does:
- With privacy mode ON (default), the bot only receives messages that:
  - Start with a slash (commands)
  - Mention the bot by username (@FaustoftheBlueRose_bot)
  - Are replies to the bot's own messages
- With privacy mode OFF, the bot receives ALL messages sent to the group.

After disabling privacy mode, you must restart the listener script for the change to take effect.

Once you have disabled privacy mode, let me know and I will restart the listener.

Note: If you ever want to re-enable privacy mode, repeat the steps and choose Enable.
