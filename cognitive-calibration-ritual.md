# Cognitive Calibration Ritual
## Water to the Rock — Daily Habit Design

---

## The Pattern You've Named

You want to build habit. Not motivation, not willpower — **habit**. The constant flow of everyday practice that gradually wears the rock smaller, like water over stone.

Writing is your water. It externalizes the fog and forces clarity.

---

## The Ritual Structure

**Twice daily. Morning and evening.**

### Morning Ritual (Intake)
*Time: [Choose a specific time — consistency matters more than perfection]*

1. **Read One Sentence**
   - Choose from something meaningful: a quote, a passage, a line that catches you.
   - Just one. Not a paragraph. One sentence.

2. **The Thought** — What does this sentence stir in you right now?

3. **The Point** — What's the core insight here? What is true?

4. **The Resolve** — What's one small thing you'll do with this today?

---

### Evening Ritual (Reflection)
*Time: [Choose a specific time — before sleep works well]*

1. **Read One Sentence**
   - Something that feels relevant to how today unfolded.
   - One sentence.

2. **The Thought** — What does this sentence mean in the context of today?

3. **The Point** — What pattern did you notice? What did you learn?

4. **The Resolve** — Tomorrow, what will you do differently?

---

## Why This Works

| Element | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| **One sentence input** | Anchors your mind. Prevents overwhelming yourself with too much material. |
| **Thought → Point → Resolve** | Forces progression from emotion → clarity → action. No stuck loops. |
| **Twice daily** | Two data points per day. Patterns emerge quickly. |
| **Specific time** | Removes the decision. Habit builds through repetition, not deliberation. |
| **Written, not thought** | Externalizes the fog. You can see what you're actually thinking. |

## What You're Actually Building

This isn't about "fixing" the pattern. It's about **collecting data on your own behavior**.

The rock shrinks when you see its true dimensions — not the size your fear imagines.

After two weeks of this ritual, you will have:
- 28 entries of what triggered you
- 28 entries of what you actually resolved
- Patterns you couldn't see before

You'll spot the **real trigger**, not the story you tell about it.
